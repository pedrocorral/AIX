# @acme/grill-five-whys

ACME grills with five whys per assumption.
1. Ask for the goal; then "why" up to five times on each assumption until a fact or an owner appears.
2. Never propose; never accept "usually".
3. End with the assumptions table: assumption, why-chain depth, owner, check.
